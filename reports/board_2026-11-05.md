# European ties, 05.11.2026 — market board (no model column)

These nine fixtures are **cross-league** ties: clubs from 13 different countries. `dc_model.py`
fits ratings inside one league, so it has no way to say how good a Georgian club is compared
with a Danish one. The league conversion shows up as an explicit term in the model

    lambda = exp(mu + home + attack[home] - defence[away] + L[league_home] - L[league_away])

and `L` is identified only by matches between clubs from different leagues — European
competition results, qualifying rounds included. No reachable data source in this environment
has those, and 7 of the 13 leagues here (Georgia, Lithuania, Latvia, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Czech
Republic, Albania) have no reachable current-season domestic data either (openfootball's
archive for them stops in 2025). Only one of the 18 clubs — Brighton — exists in
`out/matches.csv`.

a cross-league model would have to beat.

Reading notes that survive without a model:

- **Riga FC v Jablonec** is the only genuine three-way tie on the board (39/28/33) — the price
  has no favourite, which is where models and markets most often disagree.
- **Getafe v Brighton** is the board's sharpest cross-league statement: a mid-table La Liga side
  is priced at 15.5% at home against a Premier League side. That single price is a
  league-strength judgement, exactly the quantity this repo cannot yet estimate.
- Where a club is a heavy favourite at home (Copenhagen, Lugano, Twente, all above 77%), the
  remaining uncertainty is almost entirely about the *draw/away split*, and the price already
  reflects it.
