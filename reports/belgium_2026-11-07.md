## Westerlo v Charleroi — 39.4 / 23.0 / 37.6, best price home at 3.04

The only real disagreement on the board, and it comes from one rating. Westerlo have scored
25 goals in 10 games (a 1.31 attack multiplier, the third-highest in the league) behind the
leakiest defence in the division (0.81, 25 conceded - no side has conceded more); Charleroi
are 4th on 24 points with the meaner profile (attack 1.13, defence 1.16). The model sees 1.73 - 1.69 and a near coin-flip; the market sees
a clear away favourite at 2.21.

8.0pp on the home side is past the 5% value threshold, and 3.04 against a fair 2.54 is
+19.9% EV at 9.8% Kelly - on paper the biggest edge of the last three boards. It is still a
**no bet**, for two reasons that are about this league rather than this fixture:

- the market's 31.4% is 3.5pp *below* the bottom of the model's rating band, i.e. the
  disagreement is bigger than the model's own parameter error, and
- in `B1` the model's ≥5% edges have gone **212 bets, −20.26u, −9.6% ROI** against the
  closing line over the 705-match walk-forward. An edge of this shape is that model's
  signature failure mode, not its edge.

## Antwerp v Lommel SK — 31.3 / 28.9 / 39.8, market 50.6 / 24.6 / 24.8

The board's 19.3pp clash, and the honest answer is that the market is more likely to be right.

The model has Antwerp at average (attack 0.96, defence 0.99 - 12th, 10 points, four defeats in
five) hosting a Lommel side it rates as the third-best defence in the league (1.40, behind only
Club Brugge and St. Gilloise) on the back of 11 conceded in 10 games. That combination makes
the promoted club a 39.8% favourite and Antwerp a 31.3% outsider at home. The market makes
Antwerp a 50.6% favourite at 1.89 on reputation and squad, roughly 14pp above the top of the
model's home band.

Three reasons not to follow the model here, in order of weight:

1. **Lommel's rating is thin.** 10 finished top-flight matches is the entire basis for the
   1.40 defensive multiplier, and most of those came against the bottom half. The ridge
   shrinks the estimate toward average, but 10 games is 10 games.
2. **The market is the better forecast in this league on average** (+0.0133 RPS ahead of the
   model), so a disagreement of this size is a statement about the model, not about Antwerp.
3. **Antwerp's own rating is stale-ish in the other direction**: the time decay has their
   attack at 0.96 while they have scored 16 in 10, i.e. the model is punishing the 1-5 at
   Westerlo and the 0-2 at home to St. Gilloise twice over.

A 3.85 price on the away side is not a bet this repo can defend.

## Coverage caveats for this league

`B1` has been modelled since the 12-league build, and it carries the same gaps as every
non-top-5 league in this repo:

- **No xG.** 0 of 2,415 finished B1 matches have `xg_home`/`xg_away`; shots exist for 2,409 of
  them but the model consumes goals only. Every number above is a goals-only estimate.
- **No market benchmark for the current season.** Closing odds cover 2,110 of 2,415 matches
  overall but only 400 of the 705 inside the 2024-08 → 2026-11 backtest window, so the 2627
  row of the walk-forward has no `mkt RPS` at all.
- **The model loses to the close here.** 0.2084 vs 0.1951 RPS over 705 matches; 14 of the 19
  modelled leagues are in the same position (+0.005 to +0.018). That is why every verdict on
  this page is "no bet" - the correct amount to stake on a model that is behind the closing
  line is zero, and the report says so on every fixture.
- **Promoted sides are the soft spot.** Lommel SK (10 finished matches) and RAAL La Louviere
  (45) have the two shortest histories in the league; their ratings move most on a single
  result and should be read as provisional.

## Reproducing

```
python build_matches.py                       # assert_fresh() runs inside predict.py too
python backtest.py --league B1 --start 2024-08-01 --seasons 8      # -> out/backtest_B1.txt
python predict.py --league B1 --home Westerlo --away Charleroi --date 2026-11-07 \
                  --odds 3.04 3.78 2.21 --report reports/westerlo_vs_charleroi_2026-11-07.md
python predict.py --league B1 --home Leuven --away "KV Mechelen" --date 2026-11-07 \
                  --odds 2.34 3.55 2.95 --report reports/leuven_vs_mechelen_2026-11-07.md
python predict.py --league B1 --home Antwerp --away "Lommel SK" --date 2026-11-07 \
                  --odds 1.89 3.88 3.85 --report reports/antwerp_vs_lommel_2026-11-07.md
python predict.py --league B1 --home "RAAL La Louviere" --away "Cercle Brugge KSV" --date 2026-11-07 \
                  --odds 2.73 3.28 2.66 --report reports/la_louviere_vs_cercle_brugge_2026-11-07.md
```

The flashscore spellings are the input: `Leuven` → `OH Leuven`, `KV Mechelen` → `Mechelen`,
`Cercle Brugge KSV` → `Cercle Brugge` are resolved through `TEAM_ALIASES` and printed as a
`names:` line, so a name that is *not* in the table can never be silently priced from a
promotion prior.
